Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy describes how Bismon for iPhone, iPad and Mac handles information. In short: no data is collected about you by the developer.

Information the app handles

  • Bismon connects to the servers, networks and devices that you set up in the app, such as SSH, SFTP, WebDAV, SMB, NFS, VNC, RDP, Docker and Proxmox.
  • Server details, passwords, SSH keys and other settings are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are kept in your own iCloud Keychain and iCloud account, and the developer cannot see them.
  • Tailscale and WireGuard features connect your device to networks you configure. When you use Tailscale, its connection is handled under your own Tailscale account and is governed by Tailscale’s privacy policy.

What is not collected

  • No data is collected about you by the developer of Bismon.
  • Bismon has no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your servers, passwords and keys stay on your devices.
  • No data is collected by any external library or company. The only data collection is what Apple does on iOS and macOS, as described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

Website

This website does not run analytics or advertising scripts. Hosting providers may keep standard server logs, and links to other sites are governed by those sites’ own privacy policies.

Children

Bismon does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
