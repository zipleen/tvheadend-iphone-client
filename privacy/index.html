Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy describes how TvhClient for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV handles information. In short: TvhClient does not collect any personal information.

Information the app handles

  • TvhClient only talks to the tvheadend servers that you add to the app. Server addresses, login details and your preferences are stored on your device, and on your own iCloud account if you use iCloud sync between your devices.
  • Playback positions and the server list can be kept in sync between your iPhone, iPad and Apple TV through iCloud. This data stays within your Apple account; the developer cannot see it.
  • Recordings you download are stored on your device only.

What is not collected

  • No personal data is collected by the developer of TvhClient.
  • TvhClient does not send your data to the developer, to analytics services, or to advertisers.
  • No data is collected by any external library or company. The only data collection is what Apple does on iOS and tvOS, as described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

Website

This website does not run analytics or advertising scripts. Hosting providers may keep standard server logs, and links to other sites, such as GitHub, Discord or PayPal, are governed by those services’ own privacy policies.

Children

TvhClient does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
